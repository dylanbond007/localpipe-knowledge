## Step One: Who's Reading This?

There are two versions of this guide. They share the same data layer, the same deliverability work and the same copy frameworks, because none of that changes.

What changes is everything around it: who owns the domains, who works the replies, how it gets priced, and who you answer to. Pick the one that describes you, and everything after it is written for you specifically.
