# 05 · ICP and Signals

> 🎯 **What this section does.** Decides who you email, which is the highest-leverage decision in the whole system. Four layers of definition, a score, and — rebuilt from scratch for local — the list of public signals that actually exist on a business with eleven employees and no filings.

**Applies to:** Both motions for the framework. **B→L differs** on geography and on tier mix, and the differences are noted in place.

## Chapters

1. **5.1 The four layers of a local ICP** — firmographics, tools, live signals, what they value
2. **5.2 Build your ICP in 30 minutes** — from your own best and worst customers, plus the anti-ICP
3. **5.3 Score and tier your market** — the 0–100 rubric, A/B/C/D, and what each tier earns
4. **5.4 The local signal bench** — permits, second vans, review velocity, local job boards, live ads, licences, seasonality, weather
5. **5.5 Ocean state and pain research** — how saturated your category is, and the six questions to answer before writing anything
