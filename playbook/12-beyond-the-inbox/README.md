# 12 · Beyond the Inbox

> 📦 **What this section does.** The channels that compound on top of a working email program. Direct mail is the standout and it is barely used against local businesses. LinkedIn is close to useless on one-van operators and genuinely good on franchises, which is why the tier model earns its place.

**Applies to:** Mixed, and marked per chapter. 12.3 is **L→L only**. 12.1 and 12.4 apply to both.

## Chapters

1. **12.1 Direct mail to local businesses** — when it beats email, cost per touch, and QR scan routing into a real workflow
2. **12.2 The phone, LinkedIn and DMs** — layering the phone, and where LinkedIn does and does not exist
3. **12.3 Territory, proximity and density** — why a scattered list produces work you cannot profitably service
4. **12.4 Referral loops and recommendation threads** — the highest-converting channel local has, and it falls out of outbound as a side effect
