# 07 · Offer and Lead Magnet

> 🎁 **What this section does.** Builds the two offers you need: the thing you sell, and the free thing that makes a stranger give you thirty minutes. Then makes both irresistible with one tool. Written for a reader whose buyer owns the business, does the work, and signs the cheques.

**Applies to:** Both motions for the frameworks. **B→L differs** on proof and awareness level, noted in 7.1.

## Chapters

1. **7.1 Two offers, not one** — the core offer, the lead magnet, and why most people only build one
2. **7.2 The core offer gate and the value equation** — three pass/fail tests, then the four dials
3. **7.3 Reversing the risk** — the guarantee ladder, and what an owner-operator will actually believe
4. **7.4 Lead magnets for owner-operators** — the audit wins. Profile audits, review gaps, missed calls, quote response times
5. **7.5 Offer and magnet by campaign type** — matching the offer to market state and campaign motion
