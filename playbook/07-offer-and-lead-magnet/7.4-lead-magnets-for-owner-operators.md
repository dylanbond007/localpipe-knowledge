# 7.4 Lead Magnets for Owner-Operators

> 🎣 **A lead magnet is a complete solution to one narrow problem, that reveals the next one.**
>
> Complete, so it is genuinely useful on its own. Narrow, so you can deliver it in fifteen minutes. And it has to reveal something, or there is no reason to keep talking.

## 🧰 The Three Jobs a Magnet Can Do

| Job | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔍 **Reveal a problem** | Show them something broken they did not know about | An audit of something public and checkable |
| 🍰 **Give a sample** | A small, real piece of the actual work | Do one job, one page, one week, free |
| 🪜 **Do one step of the process** | Complete a stage they would otherwise have to do | Build the plan, hand it over, they can run it themselves |

## 🏆 On Local, Revealing a Problem Wins

Not always, but far more often than the other two, and for reasons specific to this buyer.

- 👀 **Local businesses have visible, checkable problems.** Their Google profile, their reviews, their website, how fast they answer the phone. All public, all inspectable without their permission
- ⏱️ **It costs you fifteen minutes** once you have done it a few times, and it costs them nothing
- 🧾 **It proves competence rather than claiming it.** You are not saying you are good, you are showing them something true they did not know
- 🎯 **It qualifies while it impresses.** If the audit comes back clean, they are not a fit, and you found out before the call

## 📚 The Local Magnet Library

Pick one. Build it once. Run it on every prospect.

- 🗺️ **Google Business Profile audit.** Missing categories, wrong hours, no photos, unclaimed listing. Enormous impact, almost never done properly
- 💬 **Review response gap.** How many reviews they have, how many they replied to, and what the unanswered negative ones are saying. Confronting and completely factual
- 📞 **Missed call analysis.** How many calls go unanswered, how fast they call back, and what that is worth in lost jobs. The most commercially direct one on this list
- 🔎 **Competitor visibility check.** Who ranks above them for the searches their customers actually use, and what those competitors are doing differently
- ⏳ **Quote response time teardown.** Submit an enquiry as a customer and time the response. Uncomfortable, effective, and the single most common reason local businesses lose work
- 🗓️ **Service area coverage map.** Where their work is clustered against where the demand is. Good for anything routed or scheduled

Choose the one that leads naturally to what you sell. A cleaning company should not be running a Google profile audit. A marketing agency absolutely should.

## 🎯 Custom Beats Automated, and AI Makes Custom Scale

A generic PDF is not a magnet. An owner-operator has no interest in a downloadable guide and never opens it.

What works is something about **their** business, with their name and their numbers in it. That used to mean one hour of work per prospect, which capped it at a handful of accounts a week.

It no longer does. The research, the checking and the first draft of the finding can all be produced at list scale. What stays human is the judgment call about what actually matters, and the conversation where you hand it over.

**The rule:** automate the gathering, keep the interpretation.

## 🎭 The Disguise Principle

The magnet should not look like a marketing asset. It should look like somebody noticed something.

- ✅ "I had a look at your Google listing and three of your service categories are missing. Happy to walk you through it, takes ten minutes"
- ❌ "Download our free 2026 Local Visibility Audit Template"

Same underlying thing. The first sounds like a person. The second sounds like a funnel, and the reader has seen a hundred of them.

## 📵 Deliver It Live, Never by Email

The most important rule on this page, and the one most people get wrong.

**If you email the audit, you have given away the only reason for the meeting.** They read it, they feel briefly informed, they do not reply, and your best asset is spent.

So:

- 📅 The email offers the finding, and asks for fifteen minutes to walk through it
- 🖥️ You deliver it on the call, screen shared, talking them through what you found
- 🔍 The conversation happens **because** you have something to show, not despite it
- 📄 They get the document afterwards, whatever happens

The exception is when you have found something urgent enough that withholding it would be unreasonable. Send that one immediately, and ask for the call anyway. Goodwill is worth more than the meeting.

## 🚫 What Does Not Work

- 📕 **Ebooks and guides.** Nobody with a van has ever downloaded one
- 🎟️ **Discounts as a magnet.** A discount is a price change, not a reason to talk. It also trains them to wait
- 📊 **Generic benchmark reports.** Interesting to you, irrelevant to somebody whose problem is next Tuesday
- 🎥 **Webinars and recorded training.** Wrong buyer. They will not give you an hour before they have given you fifteen minutes

## ✅ Before You Move On

- One magnet chosen, from the library or your own equivalent
- It reveals something about their specific business, not the category
- It takes you fifteen minutes to produce once it is systemised
- It leads naturally into what you sell
- A plan to deliver it live, not by email
