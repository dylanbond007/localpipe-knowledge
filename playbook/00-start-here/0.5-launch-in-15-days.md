# 0.5 Launch in 15 Days

> 🗓️ **You can be live in fifteen days.**
>
> Seven of those are work. Eight are waiting for domains to warm, and nothing speeds that up.
>
> Build everything in week one. Sit on your hands in week two. Launch on day fifteen.

## 🔨 Days 1 to 7: Build Everything

| Day | What you do | Covered in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Buy domains, get inboxes into warm-up | 04 |
| **2** | Campaign strategy, ICP direction, and copy | 05, 07, 08, 09 |
| **3** | Procure the rest of the tools | 13 |
| **4** | Build the lead lists | 06 |
| **5** | Set up the campaigns in the sequencer | 08, 09 |
| **6** | Post-call nurture sequences and setter scripts | 11 |
| **7** | QA the whole thing | 10.2 |

**A few notes on the order:**

- 🥇 **Day 1 is domains, always.** The warm-up clock starts here and everything else can be done while it runs
- ✍️ **Copy on day 2, list on day 4.** Deliberate. Writing first tells you which fields you actually need, so you are not paying to enrich data you were never going to use
- 📞 **Day 6 matters more than it looks.** The replies are coming. If nobody knows what to say when the phone gets picked up, the first week of leads gets wasted

## ⏳ Days 8 to 14: Ready, and Waiting

Everything is built. The only thing between you and sending is warm-up. Something you can do in the meantime:

- 🚚 **Start a second channel.** The phone and direct mail need no warm-up at all. Point them at your highest-scoring accounts now rather than in three weeks. Section 12

## 🚀 Day 15: Soft Launch

- Start at **10 to 20%** of target volume
- Watch bounce and inbox placement **daily**
- Under 2% bounce and placement holding? Climb **30 to 50%** at a time, a few days apart, until you are at full volume
- Change nothing else while you ramp

> ⚡ **Have your reply plan ready before the first reply lands.** On local, a reply is a cue to pick up the phone, and that window closes inside the hour. Section 11.

## 📈 Days 15 to 45: Ramp and Test

- Volume up first, tests second
- **Offers before messaging.** Not both at once
- **500 to 1,000 people contacted per campaign** before the numbers mean anything. Use 1,000 if your market is deep, 500 if it is tight
- Working a single metro? You will not have the volume for four parallel tests. Run them in sequence and accept slower learning. Section 8.3
- Hitting the ratios in 0.4? Stop optimising and send more

## 🔍 Day 46: Reevaluate

First look at everything with real data behind it.

- ✅ **Scale what works**, by sending more of the same rather than by improving it
- ❌ **Kill what does not**
- 🧪 **Build the next tests** from what you learned, not from what you wish had worked
- 🗺️ **Check the market depth.** Running out of people to contact? Expand contact buckets first, then adjacent categories, then geography. Section 3.3

## 🧮 Day 90: Build the Money Model

You now have real numbers, so you can replace the benchmarks with your own:

- How many people contacted per positive reply
- How many replies per booked meeting
- How many meetings held, and how many qualified
- How many closed, and at what average value

Once you have that chain you can work backwards from any revenue number to the activity it takes, and you know whether the market can support it. That is the difference between running campaigns and running a channel.

Section 03 has the model and the calculator.

## ⚠️ Where People Lose Time

Four mistakes cost more days than anything else, and all four are avoidable.

- 🏗️ **Building the list before ordering domains.** You finish a list on day two, then sit on it for a fortnight while domains warm and the addresses go stale
- 📝 **Writing copy after building the list.** You end up writing around whatever data you happened to collect, instead of collecting the data your best email needed
- 💥 **Launching at full volume the moment warm-up finishes.** Warm-up and ramp are two different things. Skipping the ramp burns the inboxes you just spent two weeks preparing
- 🔄 **Rewriting copy in week four.** Reply rate looks low, copy feels like the obvious lever, and it is almost never the problem. On local the break is in the list about three times out of four

## 🏃 If You Already Have Infrastructure

- **Warm domains and a working sequencer already running?** Skip day one. You can be live in about a week
- **Adding local to an existing outbound program?** One thing to check first. The free-provider addresses in a local list do not belong on your existing domains, and mixing them in drags down a sending reputation you spent months building. Read 4.6 before you start
