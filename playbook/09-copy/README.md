# 09 · Copy

> ✍️ **What this section does.** Writes the emails. Every framework here is standard and holds up fine. What changes is the reader — someone reading their own inbox between jobs, on a phone, who has no professional profile and no public signals — and the fact that you are writing four versions of everything, one per contact bucket.

**Applies to:** Both motions for the frameworks. **B→L differs** on proof and on awareness level, noted in 9.1 and 9.4.

## Chapters

1. **9.1 Who is actually reading** — awareness level, register, and the outcome rule
2. **9.2 The Dream Email, then dial back** — write the ceiling first, then cut to what you can afford to find
3. **9.3 The four questions and the email-1 skeleton** — line by line, with what each line is doing
4. **9.4 Personalization that works on local** — two inputs do almost all the work, and both come free with the record
5. **9.5 Copy per contact bucket** — the routing table. Four buckets, four openers, four asks
6. **9.6 The sequence, subject lines and the cut list** — three emails, hard cap, plus what to strip from every draft
7. **9.7 Annotated examples and failure modes** — worked emails across buckets and verticals, plus a deliberately bad one
