# 11 · Replies to Booked Meetings

> 📞 **What this section does.** A reply is the most expensive thing your system produces. This section turns it into a held meeting. The local-specific part is the biggest single point in the guide: on local, email is a signal channel and the conversion happens on the phone, usually within the hour.

**Applies to:** Both motions for the playbook. **L→L only** for 11.4 and 11.5 — if you have a real sales team, the handoff problem is not yours.

## Chapters

1. **11.1 The reply is a cue to dial** — the intent window, and why a positive reply is not a booked meeting
2. **11.2 The seven reply types** — one right move each, including why referrals are gold
3. **11.3 Getting them to show** — the five-step show-up engine, and no-show recovery
4. **11.4 Who works the replies** — one named person, a written response window, and what to do when the honest answer is no
5. **11.5 The setter** — workflow, script and the numbers to hold them to, for the person who makes the first call
