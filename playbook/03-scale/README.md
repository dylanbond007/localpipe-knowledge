# 03 · Scale

> 📉 **What this section does.** Works backward from a revenue number to how many emails you have to send and what it costs. Then does the thing no general outbound material does: checks whether the market physically contains that many sends.

**Applies to:** Both motions for the math. **L→L only** for the ceiling, which is the more important half.

## Chapters

1. **3.1 The funnel and the one ratio** — six stages, and leads contacted per positive reply
2. **3.2 Working backward from revenue** — the backward math, the worked example, the volume rule
3. **3.3 Market size is the ceiling, not the target** — where the funnel math meets coverage math, and what to do when it comes back short
4. **3.4 What it costs, and the calculator** — infrastructure and data cost, cost per positive reply
