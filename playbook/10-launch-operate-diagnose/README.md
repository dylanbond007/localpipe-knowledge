# 10 · Launch, Operate, Diagnose

> 🚀 **What this section does.** Takes it live without burning the domains you just paid for, keeps it running on a rhythm, and finds the real break when the numbers slip. On local the break is in the list about three times out of four, which is why the diagnostic runs bottom-up.

**Applies to:** Both motions. One change worth flagging: the pre-email fast lane is the phone and direct mail here, not LinkedIn.

## Chapters

1. **10.1 The launch timeline** — two lanes, six weeks, and what goes live in week two
2. **10.2 Pre-launch and copy QA** — the infrastructure, list, copy and tracking checks, merged into one pass
3. **10.3 The numbers to watch** — four leading, four lagging, and the good-campaign benchmark
4. **10.4 The four-pillar diagnostic** — deliverability, prospect, offer and copy, scale. In that order

## 🏢 Agency edition differences

The text above is the Business Owner Guide version, written to the person running the program. The Agency Guide version of this chapter is identical except for the 3 passages below. Use these when the work is outbound run on behalf of a client.

1. **At the top of the chapter, after the passage beginning “Applies to: Both motions. One change worth flagging: the…”, the agency edition adds:**

   > > 🏢 **Week two is where a client's patience is tested.** Nothing has sent, nothing has arrived, and they are paying. Getting the phone and direct mail lane live in week two is as much about the relationship as it is about pipeline. Say it at onboarding so the quiet fortnight is expected rather than alarming.

2. **Under “Chapters”, in place of the passage beginning “1. 10.1 The launch timeline — two lanes, six…”, the agency edition reads:**

   > 1. **10.1 The Launch Timeline.** Two lanes, six weeks, and what goes live in week two
   > 2. **10.2 Pre-Launch and Copy QA.** Infrastructure, list, copy and tracking checks, merged into one pass
   > 3. **10.3 The Numbers to Watch.** Four leading, four lagging, and which of them a client ever sees
   > 4. **10.4 The Four-Pillar Diagnostic.** Deliverability, prospect, offer and copy, scale. In that order

3. **Under “Chapters”, in place of the passage beginning “➡️ Next section: 11 · Replies to Booked Meetings…”, the agency edition reads:**

   > > ➡️ **Next section:** [11 · Replies to Booked Meetings](../11-replies-to-booked-meetings/README.md)
   > >
   > > The section that decides whether your work looks successful.
