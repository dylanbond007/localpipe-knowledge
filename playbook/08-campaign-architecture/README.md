# 08 · Campaign Architecture

> 🗂️ **What this section does.** Turns one ICP and one offer into a set of campaigns that compounds. Then confronts the problem nobody writing general outbound material has to: the standard testing protocol needs more contacts than a local market contains.

**Applies to:** Both motions for structure. **L→L only** for the testing constraint, which is the whole of 8.3. **B→L** can run the standard protocol unchanged.

## Chapters

1. **8.1 What a campaign actually is** — one segment, one offer, one person, one angle, and why blending destroys your data
2. **8.2 The local playbooks** — which of the six standard playbooks survive local, which die, and what replaces them
3. **8.3 Testing inside a finite market** — the volume rule against the coverage math, and three honest ways out

   > > 🏢 **8.3 is where your book of clients becomes an advantage.** A single local market is too small to test properly in. Three clients in the same trade is a readable sample, and that is the one structural edge you have over a business running this in-house.
