## ✅ Your Next Three Steps

If you do nothing else after the session, do these in this order.

**1. Run the market check on your next local prospect.** An hour, before you quote. Sections 3.3 and A1.2. It is the cheapest thing on this list and it prevents the most expensive mistake.

**2. Add the callback question to your intake.** Who calls, how fast, where the alert lands. A1.3 and A2.6. It is the single biggest determinant of whether your work looks successful.

**3. Hold a pre-warmed pool.** Two weeks of waiting is the biggest friction in selling a local engagement, and it is removable. Section 4.4.
