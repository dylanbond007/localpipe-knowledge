# The Agency Guide — Cold Outbound to Local Business ICPs

> 🏢 **For agencies already running outbound who want better results in local markets.**
>
> This is not a course on cold email. It assumes you can build a sequence, provision domains, read your own metrics and construct an offer. What it explains is why a playbook that works everywhere else underperforms against local businesses, and what to change.

## ⚠️ One Question First

> ### 🔀 Are You an Agency Trying to Win Clients for Yourself, Rather Than Running Outbound for Clients?
>
> If you want more meetings on **your own** calendar, and outbound is not a service you currently sell, you are a business wanting meetings rather than an agency running campaigns. Everything will make more sense in the other version.
>
> Take [**The Business Owner Guide**](../business-owner/README.md) instead, and read it as **Business → Local**, since your customers are local businesses but you are not tied to a place.
>
> **Still here?** Then you run outbound for clients, and the rest of this is written for you.

## 🎯 What Is Different in This Version

Compared to the version written for businesses running this in-house:

- **No fundamentals.** You have a method. What survives is the page on what local changes, lever by lever
- **Two sections that only exist here.** Winning the client, and running the engagement
- **A dozen shared pages that shift.** Market sizing becomes a pre-sale feasibility test, the handoff becomes a contract conversation, and the setter becomes something you sell rather than something you hire
- **One advantage you have and they do not.** You can pool test learning across several clients in the same vertical, which is the only honest way around the volume problem in a small market

## 📊 Benchmarks at a Glance

Every figure is qualified on its own page. These are the reference ranges for a well-built local list.

| Measure | Range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bounce rate** | Under 2% | The single bar for this guide. 2 to 3% investigate the list, 5% pause, 10% stop everything |
| Bounce, free-provider segment | About 1.6% | Roughly 32% higher than the rest. Workable in isolation, ugly mixed in |
| Owner name find rate | About 75% | Depends entirely on how many sources you cross-reference |
| Owner direct email find rate | About 42% | The number to size a market on |
| Combined contactable coverage | About 60% | Counts records giving a generic address but no owner-specific one |
| Owner phone find rate | About 20% | Low, and disproportionately valuable |
| Reply rate | 3 to 4% | Tracks deliverability more than data quality |
| Contacts per positive reply | 1 per 50 to 100 | The primary efficiency number |
| Monthly sends per client | 2,000 to 4,000 | Constrained by market size, not by infrastructure |
| Qualified meetings per month | 8 to 12 | At the volume above, in a healthy market |
