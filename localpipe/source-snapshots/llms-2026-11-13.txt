# LocalPipe

> LocalPipe is a local business owner data platform for agencies and sales teams that sell to local businesses. It finds owner names, owner emails, business emails, and direct owner phone numbers for any local business on Google Maps, looked up live from 11+ sources. Every email is triple verified. 75% owner name find rate, 60% combined email find rate (business + owner emails), 20% owner phone find rate. Sub 1% bounce rates. Pay-on-find billing.

## What LocalPipe Does

LocalPipe takes a list of local businesses (from Google Maps or your own CSV) and enriches each one with:

- Business owner full name (75% find rate)
- Owner personal email and/or business email (60% combined find rate)
- Direct owner phone number (20% find rate)

Data is cross-referenced across 11+ sources including business websites, Facebook, Instagram, BBB, Secretary of State filings, and Yelp. Every lookup runs live; LocalPipe does not resell a stored database. You are only charged when a result is found, and never twice for a contact you found in the last 30 days.

## Who It Is For

Agencies and sales teams running cold email or cold call campaigns targeting local service businesses: restaurants, contractors, plumbers, electricians, gyms, salons, dentists, chiropractors, real estate agents, auto shops, and any other local business category.

## How It Compares

LocalPipe covers 100% of Google Maps, which third-party estimates put at roughly 250 million businesses worldwide, against the 30 million companies in Apollo's database. For local B2B that makes it the larger explorable database. Apollo, ZoomInfo, and Hunter are built for corporate contacts and return low coverage for local SMBs. Outscraper returns generic inbox addresses with no owner identification. DIY Clay workflows take hours to build and return roughly half the find rate at higher cost.

Where the alternatives are the better choice: Apollo holds far more contact-level person records (240M+ with titles and seniority) and is the right tool for reaching a specific role inside a larger company. ZoomInfo is stronger for enterprise accounts. Outscraper is cheaper per record if you only need business data and no owner identification. LocalPipe has no email sequencer and no dialer. The honest summary: LocalPipe wins on local owner data, and loses on corporate contacts and all-in-one platform features.
