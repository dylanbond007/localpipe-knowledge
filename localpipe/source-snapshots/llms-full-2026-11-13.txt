## What is LocalPipe?

LocalPipe (https://www.localpipe.io) is a local business owner data platform for agencies and sales teams that sell to local businesses. Search any business type in any location on Google Maps, export the list, and enrich each business with the owner's name, owner email, business email and direct owner phone number, or upload your own CSV. Contacts are found live from 11+ sources, every email is triple verified, and you are only charged when a result is found.

LocalPipe is purpose-built for local, owner-operated businesses. It is not adapted from a LinkedIn database or bolted onto a generic scraper. Corporate B2B databases such as Apollo and ZoomInfo are strong for registered companies with LinkedIn presence, but most local business owners have little or none, so those databases thin out sharply on plumbers, dentists, roofers, and restaurants.

## How It Works

1. Configure your filters: pick business types and locations, then narrow results with rating and review-count filters. Preview live results before spending anything.
2. Run your export: name your list, choose an exact number of businesses or export everything available, and start the export. Results are ready in minutes.
3. Push your leads to enrichment: send exported businesses to enrichment in one click and choose what to find (owner names, emails, phone numbers).
4. Download your enriched results: a completed list with owner names, verified emails, and phone numbers, plus find-rate stats for every field, downloadable as CSV.

Behind the enrichment:
- Owner names: LocalPipe cross-references business websites, Facebook, Instagram, the Better Business Bureau, Secretary of State filings, Yelp, and other sources to identify the actual owner by name.
- Owner emails: using the owner's name, LocalPipe sources their email through a network of data partners.
- Business emails: the generic business inbox (info@, contact@) as a fallback channel.
- Owner phones: direct mobile numbers for owners, sourced through a network of data partners.
- In total, 11+ sources are cross-referenced. Enrichment runs live on demand rather than serving a stale static database.

## Find Rates and Data Quality

Average find rates on local, owner-operated businesses (the segment LocalPipe is built for; not representative of corporate B2B lists):

- Owner name find rate: 75%
- Combined email find rate (business emails + owner emails): 60%
- Owner phone find rate: 20%
- Bounce rate: users report sub 1% in live campaigns (0.11% in one documented case study)
- Emails sent using LocalPipe data: 34,700,000+

Verification: every email is triple verified before delivery, free of charge, so no separate verification step is needed before sending.

Live data: every search and enrichment runs live; LocalPipe does not resell a stored database. Contacts a user has already found are kept in that user's account for 30 days, so enriching the same business again within 30 days is never charged twice.

Homepage coverage comparison (from the October 2026 benchmark below): LocalPipe returned a verified, sendable email for 51.6% of 500 US plumbing businesses against 5.2% for Apollo, about 10x more local businesses you can actually email. Results vary by industry and region.

Data comparison benchmark (https://www.localpipe.io/data-comparison): LocalPipe, Apollo, Seamless, Hunter, and Outscraper were tested on the same 500 US plumbing businesses. Verified, sendable email coverage: LocalPipe 51.6% (258 of 500), Outscraper 12.0%, Seamless 7.0%, Apollo 5.2%, Hunter 4.5%. Direct owner mobile coverage: LocalPipe 16.6% against 4.1% for Apollo. The page also covers owner name find rate, email validity, bounce risk, and methodology. It is a vendor-run benchmark with published definitions.

International: any location indexed on Google Maps is supported. Agencies targeting Australia, the UK, Germany, Canada, and the United States have confirmed coverage. Find rates are strongest in the United States, where public record sources such as Secretary of State filings add a verification layer.

## Pricing

All plans include unlimited seats and API access. Credits are only consumed when enrichment finds a result (pay on find).

Credit costs:
- Business email (e.g. info@business.com): 1 credit per found email
- Owner email (e.g. dan@business.com): 2 credits per found email
- Owner phone: 4 credits per found phone
- Owner name discovery: included on every credit plan (no credits, no name limit)

LocalPipe has no free plan. Plans start at $57/month.

Monthly plans:
- Starter: $57/month. 5,000 credits and 10,000 business exports per month. For reaching out to 1K to 5K companies a month.
- Growth (most popular): $97/month. 10,000 credits and 20,000 business exports per month. For reaching out to 5K to 10K companies a month.
- Pro: $157/month. 20,000 credits and 40,000 business exports per month. For reaching out to 10K to 15K companies a month.

Each credit plan includes: credits, business owner name finding, business exports (the number of businesses you can export from the Google Maps search), API access, unlimited seats, unlimited credit rollover (unused credits carry over with no limit), and priority support.

What 5,000 credits buys (Starter, one month): 5,000 business emails, or 2,500 owner emails, or 1,250 owner phones, or any mix.

Annual billing (Starter, Growth, Pro): 40% off, billed once a year, with all 12 months of credits and business exports given upfront.
- Starter: $34/month billed yearly (60,000 credits and 120,000 business exports upfront)
- Growth: $58/month billed yearly (120,000 credits and 240,000 business exports upfront)
- Pro: $94/month billed yearly (240,000 credits and 480,000 business exports upfront)

Usage-Based plan: $97/month plus extra usage, for reaching out to 15K+ companies a month. Monthly billing only; the annual discount does not apply. It works like OpenAI billing: you have a balance that is drawn down as you run enrichments, it tops up automatically when it falls below a threshold, and the $97 subscription is added to your balance every month. Per-find prices:
- Owner name finds: $0.005 per find
- Business emails: $0.005 per email
- Owner emails: $0.015 per email
- Phone numbers: $0.03 per number
- Google Maps scraping: $1 per 1,000 businesses
Includes: pay on successful find only, unlimited enrichment volume, already-found results are free, auto top-ups, and API access.

Refunds: credits are only charged when a result is found. Plan fees are non-refundable, including for lists with lower-than-expected match rates, except as provided in the Terms of Service.

## Features

- Google Maps Business Search: search any business type in any city, state, or country, and filter by rating, review count, website presence, and price level.
- Owner Name Discovery: identifies the actual owner by name from live sources (websites, Facebook, Instagram, BBB, Secretary of State filings).
- Direct Owner Emails: the owner's own inbox rather than a generic info@ address.
- Direct Owner Phone Numbers: direct mobile numbers, so you can skip the front desk.
- Custom Title Waterfalling: search for specific titles in priority order (for example Equipment Manager, then Owner, then Maintenance Director). Raises find rates for hard-to-find roles from roughly 50% to 75%.
- Pay on Find Credits: credits are only consumed when enrichment returns a result; Usage-Based balances top up automatically.
- Data Recovery Panel: if an API call or Clay table fails, enriched data can be recovered from the dashboard, and no credits are lost to technical failures.
- API and Clay: an HTTP API with webhook callbacks and a ready-made Clay template. One API call replaces a seven-step, five-table Clay enrichment flow. About 90% of high-volume users run entirely through the API.

| Triple email verification | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Setup time | Seconds | 45 min+ | Minutes | Minutes | 6+ hours |
| Pay on find billing | Yes | No | No | No | No |

Where the alternatives are the better choice:
- Apollo holds far more contact-level person records (with titles and seniority) and includes a built-in sequencer. It is the right tool for reaching a specific role inside a larger company. If your list is corporate rather than local, start with Apollo or ZoomInfo.
- Outscraper is cheaper per raw record. It returns every email visible on a website with no distinction between a generic inbox and the decision maker, and you pay for rows whether or not a usable contact is found. It suits teams that want maximum volume at the lowest unit cost and will clean the output themselves.
- Clay is the most flexible if you want to build and control your own enrichment waterfall, at the cost of setup time and per-step credits.

What LocalPipe does not do: it has no email sequencer and no dialer, and it does not send email. It exports to CSV or pushes into your existing sequencer (Instantly, Smartlead, and similar) through Clay or the API, so you keep your sending setup and sender reputation. There is no free plan; plans start at $57/month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is LocalPipe?
A: A local business lead generation and contact enrichment platform for agencies and sales teams. Search any local business worldwide, export lead lists, and enrich them with owner names, direct emails, and phone numbers, or import your own CSV.

Q: How does the credit system work?
A: Business emails cost 1 credit, owner and decision maker emails cost 2 credits, and owner phone numbers cost 4 credits. Owner name discovery is included on every credit plan. Credits are only consumed when enrichment finds a result, and unused credits roll over with no limit on Starter, Growth, and Pro.

Q: How accurate is the data?
A: On local, owner-operated businesses: 75% owner name find rate, 60% combined email find rate, and 20% owner phone find rate on average. Users report sub 1% bounce rates in live campaigns.

Q: How many owner emails should I expect from 1,000 local businesses?
A: Roughly 750 owner names, about 600 emails across business and owner addresses combined, and about 200 owner phones is a realistic outcome. Expect a partial return rather than a full one.

Q: Why not just use Apollo or ZoomInfo?
A: They are built on LinkedIn and corporate web data and are strong for registered companies, but most local business owners have little or no LinkedIn presence. In LocalPipe's internal benchmarking on local businesses, Apollo returned an owner name for roughly 20% of records against LocalPipe's 75%. For corporate lists, Apollo or ZoomInfo is the better starting point.

Q: How is LocalPipe different from Outscraper?
A: Outscraper is cheaper per raw record but returns every email on a site with no owner identification, billed per row. LocalPipe identifies the owner by name and returns the best contact for that person, and misses cost nothing.

Q: Can I use LocalPipe with Clay?
A: Yes, through the HTTP API with webhook callbacks and a ready-made Clay template.

Q: What happens if an API call fails?
A: Enriched data is recoverable from the Data Recovery panel, and no credits are lost to technical failures.

Q: Do you support international searches?
A: Yes, any location indexed on Google Maps. Find rates are strongest in the United States.

Q: Is there a refund policy?
A: Fees are non-refundable except as expressly provided in the Terms of Service.
