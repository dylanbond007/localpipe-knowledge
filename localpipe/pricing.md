# LocalPipe pricing

Prices change. Before quoting a price to a customer, check https://www.localpipe.io/#pricing.

## The short version

- **No free plan.** Plans start at $57 a month.
- Three credit plans (Starter, Growth, Pro) plus a Usage-Based plan for high volume.
- **Pay on find.** Credits are spent only when a result comes back. A miss costs nothing.
- You are never charged twice for a contact you found in the last 30 days.
- Every plan includes owner name finding, API access and unlimited seats.

## Credit costs

| What you get | Credits (Starter, Growth, Pro) | Price on Usage-Based |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner name | Included, no credits, no monthly limit | $0.005 per find |
| Business email (info@, contact@) | 1 credit per found email | $0.005 per email |
| Owner email (the owner's own inbox) | 2 credits per found email | $0.015 per email |
| Owner phone (direct number) | 4 credits per found phone | $0.03 per number |
| Google Maps businesses | Included in the plan's monthly exports | $1 per 1,000 businesses |

Searching Google Maps, exporting businesses and finding owner names are covered by the plan's allowances. Credits are used only for contact enrichment.

| **Starter** | $57 | $34/month | 5,000 | 10,000 | Reaching 1K to 5K companies a month |
| **Growth** (most popular) | $97 | $58/month | 10,000 | 20,000 | Reaching 5K to 10K companies a month |
| **Pro** | $157 | $94/month | 20,000 | 40,000 | Reaching 10K to 15K companies a month |
| **Usage-Based** | $97 + usage | Monthly only | Pay per find | $1 per 1,000 businesses | Reaching 15K+ companies a month |

Each credit plan includes: credits, owner name finding, Google Maps exports, API access, unlimited seats, **unlimited credit rollover** (unused credits carry over with no limit) and priority support.

### Annual billing

40% off Starter, Growth and Pro, billed once a year, with all 12 months of credits and exports added upfront.

| Plan | Per month, billed yearly | Credits upfront | Exports upfront |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $34 | 60,000 | 120,000 |
| Growth | $58 | 120,000 | 240,000 |
| Pro | $94 | 240,000 | 480,000 |

The annual discount does not apply to Usage-Based.

### Usage-Based

$97 a month plus usage, monthly billing only. It works like a prepaid balance: the $97 subscription is added to the balance each month, finds are drawn from it at the per-find prices above, and it tops up automatically when it falls below a threshold. Includes pay on successful find only, unlimited enrichment volume, already-found results free, auto top-ups and API access.

Worked example from the site: 15,000 businesses at average find rates comes to roughly **$300 for the month**. About $56 for 11,250 owner names, $135 for 9,000 owner emails, $90 for 3,000 phones and $15 for scraping 15,000 businesses, with the $97 subscription counted toward that total.

## Sizing a plan to a list

Real campaigns mix contact types. These estimates use the average find rates (60% email, 20% phone) and are deliberately conservative: they price every found email as an owner email at 2 credits. When the fallback returns a business email it costs 1 credit, so real usage is usually lower.

| Businesses per month | Emails found (60%) | Owner phones found (20%) | Credits for emails + phones | Plan that fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | ~60 | ~20 | ~200 (emails only: ~120) | Starter |
| 500 | ~300 | ~100 | ~1,000 | Starter |
| 2,500 | ~1,500 | ~500 | ~5,000 | Starter (Growth for headroom) |
| 5,000 | ~3,000 | ~1,000 | ~10,000 | Growth |
| 10,000 | ~6,000 | ~2,000 | ~20,000 | Pro |
| 15,000+ | ~9,000 | ~3,000 | Pay per find | Usage-Based |

Rules of thumb:

- Plan for about **2 credits per business** enriched for emails and phones together, or about **1.2 credits per business** for emails only.
- A 5,000-business list needs about 6,000 credits for emails alone, which fits Growth with room to spare. Adding owner phones on the same list adds about 4,000 credits.
- Email-first teams get the most from each plan. If calling is the main channel, budget for phones on Growth or Pro, or use Usage-Based at $0.03 per number.
- If the year's volume is known, yearly billing cuts the price 40% and puts all 12 months of credits in the account upfront, which helps with uneven months.

## Testing coverage without a free plan

There is no free plan and no free trial credits. Starter is the cheapest way to test a niche: a 100-business test uses about 200 credits for emails and phones, or about 120 for emails only, out of 5,000. Run a city and category you actually plan to sell into and look at how many owner emails come back. The site's own advice is to bring 50 to a few hundred target businesses and compare what returns.

## Refunds

Credits are charged only when a result is found. Plan fees are non-refundable, including for lists with lower-than-expected match rates, except as expressly provided in the Terms of Service (https://www.localpipe.io/legal).

## Discount codes

There is no active discount code. The Local Legend System's reader code `LOCALLEGEND` is retired (confirmed by Dylan on 13 November 2026). Do not offer it, or any other discount, unless one is published on the site.

| Tool type | Examples | How you pay | What you get back |
