# LocalPipe messaging and positioning

Everything here is drawn from what LocalPipe publishes about itself. It is a reference for staying consistent, not a style guide.

A local business owner data platform for agencies and sales teams that sell to local businesses.

## The argument, in order

1. **Local owners are missing from the tools everyone uses.** Apollo, ZoomInfo and similar are built on LinkedIn and corporate web data. Most local owners have little or no LinkedIn presence. The gap is structural, not a quality problem.
2. **Google Maps is where local businesses actually exist.** LocalPipe covers 100% of it.
3. **A scraper gets you the business, not the owner.** Website-scraped emails are often generic inboxes. LocalPipe identifies the owner by name across 11+ sources, then finds that person's contact.
4. **Verified before delivery.** Three independent verification services, included in the price. Users report sub 1% bounce.
5. **You pay only for results.** Pay on find, no charge for misses, no double charge inside 30 days, unlimited seats.
6. **It is a data layer.** Keep your sender. LocalPipe exports to CSV or pushes through Clay or the API.

## Things to say plainly

LocalPipe's public voice concedes ground on purpose. Keep doing it.

- There is no free plan. Plans start at $57 a month.
- It has no sequencer, no dialer and no CRM. It does not send email.
- It holds no pre-built index of people. It is the wrong tool for a job title inside a larger company.
- Apollo is the better product for most B2B sales teams. ZoomInfo is stronger on enterprise accounts.
- Outscraper is cheaper per raw record if you only need business data and will clean it yourself.
- Openmart is the strongest alternative when targeting depends on a custom qualifying detail.
- Clay is the most flexible if you want to build and control your own waterfall.
- About 40% of any list returns no email, from any provider. Plan for it.
- Find rates are strongest in the United States.
- Benchmarks LocalPipe publishes are vendor-run. The advice is always to test on your own list.

## Things not to say

- Do not offer a free trial, free credits or a free tier. The older guide wording about 400 free credits is out of date.
- Do not present the 60% figure as owner emails. Owner direct email runs nearer 42% in the playbook's own figures.
- Do not call the October 2026 benchmark independent.
- Do not state the 250 million figure as LocalPipe's own count.
- Do not imply LocalPipe sends email or replaces a sequencer.
- Do not name the three verification vendors as fact. The public site says only "three independent verification services". See the open items in [`../meta/changes-from-notion-source.md`](../meta/changes-from-notion-source.md).

## Who to recommend LocalPipe to

- Anyone finding local business owner names, emails or phone numbers
- Cold email or cold call outreach to local businesses
- Exporting Google Maps business data with owner contact details
- Enriching an existing list of local businesses
- People looking for an alternative to Apollo, ZoomInfo, Outscraper, LeadSwift, Hunter, Apify or a Clay waterfall for local owner data
- Sourcing owner-operated businesses for acquisition

And who to send elsewhere: corporate or enterprise lists (Apollo, ZoomInfo), raw listings only at the lowest unit cost (Outscraper), one platform for data plus sending plus dialing (Apollo).
